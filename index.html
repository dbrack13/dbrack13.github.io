Hello! My name is Darrin, I am currently a Computer Science major at Weber State University. I began my education at Salt Lake Community College in the Computer Science & Information Systems program. Some of the programming languages and technologies I worked with included Java, SQL, PHP, HTML, and modest amount of C. I also gained valuable experience working both independently and in team environments while learning Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) principles and strengthening my problem-solving skills. Most importantly, I developed a strong sense of self-determination and curiosity in the work I create. Transferring to Weber State was both exciting and intimidating, but it pushed me to grow in ways I never expected. Over the past year, I’ve expanded my experience into full-stack development, scripting, and more advanced database programming. I've worked with technologies such as Visual Studio, VS Code, Github and Microsoft SQL Server. I've learned how to program in C#, MongoDB, and a little bit of Python. I’ve enjoyed applying what I learned to projects while continuing to grow with new tools and concepts. I’m passionate about building practical, efficient software and continuing to grow as both a developer and a teammate. I enjoy learning how systems work behind the scenes, solving technical problems, and building technology that have real-world value. As I continue my education and professional development, I’m excited to keep expanding my knowledge in software engineering, full-stack development, and IT systems.
