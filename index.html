Aug 2026 - Dec 2026

Overview

A diagnostic system for a ROS 2 RC car built on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin.

Details

A web dashboard shows the health of the LiDAR, camera, VESC, driver, battery, and battery and computer temperatures, and is being extended with remote operation and camera and LiDAR views. A companion PCB mounts on the Orin with 10 diagnostic LEDs for quick-glance status, programmable LEDs for student experiments, a 3-digit battery voltage display, and an 8×8 LED matrix hooked up to the LiDAR to show how close objects are to the car.
